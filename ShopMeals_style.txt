SHOP MEALS FORMAT SPECIFICATION
================================
Use this exact format when writing Factor / HelloFresh meals for Panther.
Paste this into Shop → Import Meals.

You can give this file + your meal list to any AI and say:
"Convert these meals to this exact format."


RULES
-----

1. BRAND starts with:   # FACTOR | Week label
                        # HELLOFRESH | Week label
2. MEALS start with:    - Meal Name | 620 cal | 34p | 30f | 50c | headline: ... | category: ...

   Fields after the name are separated by |
   Name is required. Everything else is optional.

3. Lines starting with // are comments (ignored)
4. Empty lines are ignored
5. You can put BOTH brands in one paste. The app splits them.


NUTRIENT FIELDS
---------------
Use either the short form or the labeled form.

  620 cal          or   cal: 620
  34p              or   pro: 34
  30f              or   fat: 30
  50c              or   carb: 50
  8fib             or   fib: 8
  6sat             or   sat: 6
  22sug            or   sug: 22
  70chol           or   chol: 70
  730na            or   sod: 730

  headline: with Potatoes & Greens
  category: Seafood

Anything that is not a nutrient or headline/category is treated as the headline.


COMPLETE EXAMPLE
----------------

# FACTOR | Week of Oct 20

- Salmon Burger & Collard Greens | 620 cal | 34p | 30f | 50c | 8fib | 6sat | 22sug | 70chol | 730na | headline: with sweet potatoes | category: Seafood
- Chicken Alfredo | 710 cal | 42p | 28f | 55c | 4fib | category: Poultry
- Beef Chili | cal: 540 | pro: 38 | fat: 22 | carb: 40 | fib: 9 | category: Beef

# HELLOFRESH | Week of Oct 20

- Mozzarella & Herb Chicken | 760 cal | 53p | 36f | 58c | 7fib | 14sat | 12sug | 185chol | 650na | headline: with roasted carrots | category: Poultry
- Moo Shu Pork Bowls | 880 cal | 29p | 39f | 97c | 3fib | headline: with cabbage & rice | category: Pork


TIPS
----
- Copy numbers off the Factor / HelloFresh card or the nutrition PDF
- Only list the meals you actually ordered (usually 7 each)
- Import replaces that brand's list, then pick your 7 in Shop
- Fuel uses these macros when you check a meal off
